Who I Am
About Me

I started in science — a B.Tech in Biotechnology, then an MSc in Molecular Medicine from Sheffield. I didn't plan to end up in sales. But hypothesis-driven thinking, comfort with complexity, and genuine curiosity about how things work turned out to be a better foundation for GTM than most sales training I've seen.

Over the last decade I've worked across B2B sales, customer success, and co-founding — across India and Canada — in healthcare, SaaS, and optics. Now at Workhall, I'm building the GTM motion for a US market push: ICP architecture, AI-assisted research, outbound strategy, and positioning.

The philosophy hasn't changed: listen well, ask sharper questions, make the next step obvious. The tools have just gotten better.
